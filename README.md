# agentvault-assignment
Business Development Internship Assignment – AgentVault
1. Identify 2 Target User Segments
Target User Segment 1: Crypto Traders
Why them? Crypto traders actively look for tools that help them analyze the market faster and make better trading decisions. Since AgentVault is an AI-powered crypto trading assistant, this audience is the ideal early adopter.
Pain Point
Tracking the crypto market 24/7 is difficult.
Too much information from different platforms.
Making quick trading decisions can be stressful.
Where would I find them?
X (Twitter)
Discord crypto communities
Telegram crypto groups
Reddit (r/CryptoCurrency)
Binance & Bybit communities
Target User Segment 2: University Blockchain Clubs & Student Investors
Why them? Students interested in blockchain and Web3 enjoy trying new products and often become early adopters. They can also spread the product within their communities.
Pain Point
Lack of practical AI tools for learning crypto trading.
Limited access to new Web3 products.
Need for affordable and beginner-friendly solutions.
Where would I find them?
LinkedIn
Discord
University Blockchain Clubs
Web3 student communities
Hackathons and campus events
2. Outreach Example
LinkedIn / Twitter Message
Hi!
I came across your profile and noticed your interest in crypto and Web3.
We're currently launching AgentVault, an AI-powered crypto trading assistant, and we're inviting a small group of early users to try it before the public launch.I'd love to offer you early access in exchange for your honest feedback. If you're interested, I'd be happy to share the details.
Looking forward to hearing from you!
Best, Shreya
3. GTM (Go-To-Market) Ideas
Idea 1: University Blockchain Club Partnership
Partner with blockchain clubs in colleges and offer exclusive early access. Organize short online demo sessions and encourage students to test the product and share feedback.
Why I Chose These Strategies
These user groups are active, curious about new Web3 products, and likely to provide valuable feedback. The suggested campaigns are low-cost, easy to execute, and can help AgentVault acquire its first 50 users quickly while building awareness in the crypto community.
